While linear transportation models (`SCIP`) assign optimal bulk volume between origin-destination pairs, they do not order stops. If applied to physical delivery trucks, naive unguided dispatch yields deadhead return mileage and misses tight customer receiving windows. Our pywrapcp.RoutingModel constructs multi-stop loops (`Depot ➔ A ➔ B ➔ Depot`) that simultaneously respect Truck TEU Limits (`AddDimensionWithVehicleCapacity`) and strict Delivery Time Windows (`AddDimension`).
